# About direction contract

## Route job

`/about` must make Mina Forma's human posture, working method and material restraint credible to a visitor who already understands the offer or has seen the work.

## Direction

- **Audience moment:** the visitor is checking who the studio is and how collaboration feels before starting a project.
- **Memorable truth:** Mina Forma shapes spaces around people, daily work and clear material decisions.
- **Primary action:** `START A CONVERSATION`.
- **First three eye stops:** the About identity and statement; the human working-table evidence; the concise studio posture.
- **Sibling relationship:** Home positions the studio, Services indexes the offer, Projects indexes proof, About explains the studio's posture and way of working.
- **Anti-pattern:** a Home-like split hero with a large photo beside the headline; an equal card grid; a decorative biography; a second services process page.

## Section map

1. Compact shared header with `About` active.
2. Image-free editorial masthead: `ABOUT MINA FORMA`, `SPACES WITH A HUMAN MEASURE.` and one supporting paragraph.
3. Wide human working-table photograph beginning inside the first viewport, with a quiet caption rail.
4. Manifesto and material logic: `A QUIETER WAY TO BUILD PRESENCE` plus one restrained material strip.
5. Studio method and story: four flat method bands beside a compact linear timeline.
6. Principles: three unequal editorial rows, not cards.
7. Human-process closing image and `START A CONVERSATION` action.
8. Shared compact footer.

## Content budget

- Masthead: one eyebrow, one three-line maximum headline, one paragraph, no CTA.
- Working evidence: one dominant photograph and one short label/caption.
- Manifesto: one heading and one concise paragraph.
- Method: four verbs with one sentence each.
- Story: four dates with one sentence each.
- Principles: three headings with one short paragraph each.
- Closing: one headline, one paragraph and one button.

## Primary evidence

Human hands working over plans and material samples. Photography proves the studio posture; it does not decorate the masthead.

## Elementor map

- Header/footer: shared Theme Builder templates; WordPress Nav Menu for real navigation.
- Masthead: Containers, Heading and Text Editor widgets only.
- Wide working image: one Image widget in a fixed landscape container; caption remains editable text outside the bitmap.
- Manifesto/material strip: two-column Container with Text Editor and separate Image widgets.
- Method/story: nested Containers with Heading/Text Editor fields and CSS rules; no icon-card component.
- Principles: three flat Containers with editable Heading/Text Editor content; no rasterized copy.
- Closing: Image widget, Heading, Text Editor and Button.

## Treatment budgets

- **Border contract:** outer rails and horizontal section separators are mandatory; one frame may own the primary working image; no decorative box around every text group.
- **Shadow budget:** zero interface shadows; only natural photographic contact shadows.
- **Texture budget:** subtle paper field globally; tactile material texture concentrated in the manifesto band and photography.
- **Image density:** one strong photograph per viewport maximum; four strong visual units across the complete page.
- **CTA emphasis:** the orange closing button owns high contrast; cyan is limited to the active navigation underline and short technical rules.

## Locked text

Header: `Mina Forma`, `Studio`, `Services`, `Projects`, `Method`, `About`, `Contact`, `PLAN YOUR PROJECT`.

Masthead:

- `ABOUT MINA FORMA`
- `SPACES WITH A HUMAN MEASURE.`
- `We shape commercial interiors with restraint, material honesty, and operational clarity.`

Manifesto:

- `A QUIETER WAY TO BUILD PRESENCE`
- `We believe good spaces don't try to impress. They support what matters. Our work starts with listening—deeply—to your business, your people, and how you operate. From that understanding, we shape interiors that feel timeless, work effortlessly, and grow with you.`

Method: `LISTEN`, `SHAPE`, `SPECIFY`, `SUPPORT`.

Story markers: `2018`, `2020`, `2023`, `NOW`.

Principles: `USE MATERIALS HONESTLY`, `DESIGN FOR DAILY WORK`, `LEAVE ROOM FOR CHANGE`.

Closing:

- `FROM FIRST CONVERSATION TO WORKING SPACE`
- `A human process from start to finish. We collaborate closely, share clearly, and build spaces that truly work—for your team and your clients.`
- `START A CONVERSATION`

## Prohibited drift

- No Home-style split-image hero or Home service/project card preview.
- No products, plugins, quote tools, ecommerce, 3D viewer or dashboard UI.
- No rounded cards, gradients, floating decoration, fake metrics or invented navigation.
- No oversized photo that pushes the masthead message or first evidence below the target fold.
- No fragile absolute positioning; the composition must map to Elementor containers and grids.
