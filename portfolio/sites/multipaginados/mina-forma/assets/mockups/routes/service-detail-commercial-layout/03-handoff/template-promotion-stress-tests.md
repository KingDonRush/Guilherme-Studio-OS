## Tests

1. `Interior Concept`: **passed 2026-07-15** — concept, material and lighting evidence preserved the template skeleton.
2. `Implementation Package`: **passed 2026-07-15** — the longest title and document-heavy evidence preserved the template skeleton.
3. Responsive/content resilience: confirm fixed media slots, optional related case and stable repeated-item counts before Theme Builder promotion.

## Pass

- Same section grammar works without filler.
- Masthead geometry survives short and long titles.
- Primary evidence changes medium without changing hierarchy.
- All content remains dynamic and Elementor-feasible.
- No service requires a bespoke full-page composition.

## Fail

- A service needs a Home-like hero or a different section order.
- Fixed four-item groups force repeated or weak content.
- Images grow parent sections or require absolute positioning.
- Service-specific evidence becomes decorative instead of explanatory.
