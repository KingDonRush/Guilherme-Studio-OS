# Interior Concept — Single Service stress test

## Test job

Prove that the approved Commercial Layout composition can express `Interior Concept` without becoming generic, changing its section grammar or forcing plan-specific content.

## Protected template invariants

- Compact photo-free service masthead.
- Orange service code beside a condensed title.
- Primary evidence immediately after the masthead.
- Flat four-outcome and four-step bands.
- Asymmetric deliverables evidence.
- One related project, shared CTA and shared footer.
- Continuous rails, flat separators, zero UI shadows and fixed media slots.

## Allowed substitutions

- Service number, title, thesis, CTA and copy.
- Primary evidence medium: concept board instead of floor plan.
- Callouts, outcomes, steps and deliverable labels.
- Related project and its individual media.

## Locked content

- `SERVICE / 01`
- `INTERIOR CONCEPT`
- `CONCEPT DIRECTION FOR MATERIALS, LIGHT AND ATMOSPHERE.`
- `DEFINE THE DIRECTION`
- `CONCEPT AS EVIDENCE`
- `THE ROOM SHOULD FEEL INTENTIONAL BEFORE IT IS SPECIFIED.`
- Callouts: `BRAND SIGNAL`, `MATERIAL LOGIC`, `LIGHTING MOOD`, `VISUAL LANGUAGE`
- Outcomes: `DIRECTION`, `COHERENCE`, `ATMOSPHERE`, `DECISION SPEED`
- Process: `BRIEF THE INTENT`, `BUILD THE LANGUAGE`, `TEST THE PALETTE`, `ISSUE THE CONCEPT`
- Deliverables: `CONCEPT BOARD`, `MATERIAL PALETTE`, `LIGHTING NOTES`, `VISUAL DIRECTION`
- Related case: `CLÍNICA LUME`
- CTA: `DEFINE THE SPACE BEFORE DETAILS TAKE OVER.` and `PLAN YOUR PROJECT`

## Elementor map

The existing Single Service widgets remain unchanged: dynamic Heading/Text/Button fields, repeated outcome/process groups, individual Image widgets, Grid Containers and shared Theme Builder surfaces. Only field values and raster assets change.

## Pass condition

The result reads immediately as Interior Concept while remaining visibly the same Single Service system. No section may grow, split or become a new hero recipe.
