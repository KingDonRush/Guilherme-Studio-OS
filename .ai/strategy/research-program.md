# Research Program

## Goal

Create a grounded strategy for international WordPress jobs using both lived
reports and current market data.

## Friend Reports

Collect structured notes from friends who got international work:
- role title;
- country or hiring market;
- how they got the lead;
- portfolio/GitHub signals that mattered;
- interview process;
- rate or salary range if they are comfortable sharing;
- English level expected;
- red flags they learned to avoid;
- advice they would repeat.

## Internet Research

Research should answer:
- what WordPress/Elementor roles ask for in 2026;
- how often plugin development appears as a differentiator;
- which portfolio proof gets attention;
- expected English and communication standards;
- common rates for freelance and full-time work;
- where candidates are being found.

## Output

Keep raw notes in `docs/research-log.md`.
Condense strategy updates into `docs/portfolio-strategy.md`.
Record durable agent assumptions in `.ai/memory/assumptions.md`.
